Company Profile

About Monarchy

Started in 1984 by a police family. Now run by the second generation, with 100 guards posted and more on standby.

How we started

Our late father was a Police Lieutenant with the Western Police District. In 1984, together with fellow retired officers, he put up a security agency. It was registered with the DTI and licensed by PNP SAGSD, Camp Crame, and became a member of PADPAO under PCSUSIA.

Our officers came from the PNP and the military. Most rose from the ranks. Their work covered intelligence, crime prevention, and case solution.

By the early 2000s, we were already posting guards for VIPs and industrial plants. We handled burglary, theft, sabotage, and bomb awareness concerns.

When our father passed, his son took over to continue the work. He is an Army reservist sergeant with MBA, CSMS, and BOSH-SO2 training. The company was renamed Monarchy Security Agency. Our current license is PNP-SOSIA No. PSA-WGS-000200-2024.

Where we are now

We have 100 guards posted at present. When clients need more, we pull from licensed relievers through our partner training academy. We also invest in basic security equipment to support our guards on post.
