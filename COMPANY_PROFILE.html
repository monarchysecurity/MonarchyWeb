Company Profile

About Monarchy

Started in 1984 by a police family. Now run by the second generation, with 100 guards posted and more on standby.

How we started

Inspired by my father, A late Police Lieutenant WPD (Ret) his old Security Agency, was organized by a core group of Retired Organic Western Police District Officers, since 1984 my father was operating an security agency and was duly registered as single proprietorship APPROVED BY THE DEPARTMENT OF Trade and Industry and was given licensed to operate by PNP SAGSD, Camp Crame with Department of trade and Industry member of Philippine Association of Detective and Protective Agency Operator (PADPAO) under supervision of Philippine Association of Detective and Protective Agency Operators, inc (PCSUSIA). As the agency began it operation, my father appointed as set of retired PNP and Military Officers, whore rose from the ranks with a vast spectrum of training and experiences, specializing in intelligence, detection, prevention and solution of criminal activities. Furthermore, our staff members are accomplished officers with parallel background and training of security and intelligence, well enough to enable the corporation in addressing the needs of our clients as far as protective intelligence and security are concerned nationwide. My father Security Agency then emerged as one of the pioneers in VIP and Industrial Security in early part of year 2000 and remain more focused in addressing the protection of our Clients from the danger of day-to-day crimes, ie. Burglary, theft, business sabotage and furthermore, Bomb detection and control. My Father Late Police Lieutenant WPD (Ret) left his Security Agency, to continue his legacy I his son a member of Philippine Eagle a Sergeant of Reservist under Philippine Army taking up MBA, CSMS and BOSH-SO2 took his placed, my father's Security Agency was reconstituted as Monarchy Security Agency under the name of my sister in law and now under my leadership and renewed by PNP-SOSIA, Camp Crame License to Operate No. PSA-WGS-000200-2024. Today, MONARCHY Security Agency, has a total posted guards of One Hundred (100) which were absorbed from old security agency and maintains a list of duly licensed security personnel, made available by our tie-up Security Training Academy, under accredited by the owner, ready for deployment in case the needs arise. MONARCHY Security Agency Management believes that security manpower and modern security technology must compliment with each other in order to deliver the quality security service to the client. Thus this company is starting to invest on modern security system and technologies that would support our security personnel at the field as well as for the clients use. A percentage of Investments are regularly channeled to training and professional development of our staff and security personnel.

Where we are now

We have 100 guards posted at present. When clients need more, we pull from licensed relievers through our partner training academy. We also invest in basic security equipment to support our guards on post.
